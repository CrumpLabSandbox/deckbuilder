Euchre

Cheat sheet. Four players, two partnerships sitting opposite. First team to 10 wins.

Deck and deal

24 cards: 9, 10, J, Q, K, A of each suit. Deal 5 to each player (groups of 2 and 3 is traditional). Turn the top of the remaining four face up. The deal passes to the left each hand.

Trump order (example: hearts are trump)

J♥right bower J♦left bower A♥K♥Q♥10♥9♥

The right bower is the jack of trump. The left bower is the other jack of the same colour, and for this hand it is a trump, not a card of its printed suit. Every trump beats every non-trump. In the other suits the order is A, K, Q, J, 10, 9 (the left bower's suit is missing its jack).

Bidding

  1. Round 1: starting left of the dealer, each player says "order it up" (the turned card's suit becomes trump) or "pass". The dealer says "pick it up" or passes. If trump is made, the dealer takes the turned card into hand and discards one face down.
  2. Round 2: if everyone passed, turn the card down. Starting left of the dealer, each player may name another suit or pass.
  3. If everyone passes again, either the dealer must name a suit (stick the dealer) or the cards are thrown in and the next player deals. Agree which before you start.

The team that chose trump are the makers; the other team are the defenders. Whoever chooses trump may say "alone": their partner lays down their cards and sits out.

Play

  • The player left of the dealer leads (skip a player who is sitting out). The winner of each trick leads the next.
  • Follow suit if you can. The left bower is trump: if trump is led and it's your only trump, you must play it; if its printed suit is led, it does not count.
  • If you can't follow, play any card. Trumping in is allowed but never required.
  • Highest trump wins; with no trump, the highest card of the suit led wins.

Scoring

Traditionally each team keeps score with a 6 and a 4 from the unused cards, uncovering pips as points are scored.

Bidding rules of thumb

  • Three trump, or two strong trump plus an off-suit ace, is usually enough.
  • Count the bowers as your best cards; a hand with both bowers is strong.
  • Ordering up an opponent dealer hands them a trump. Your partner as dealer gets it instead.
  • Go alone with both bowers (or right bower and ace) plus a third trump and at most one weak side card.

Playing rules of thumb

  • Makers: lead trump to pull the opponents' trump.
  • Defenders: lead off-suit aces, not trump.
  • If your partner is winning the trick, play low.
  • Win as cheaply as you can; save high cards.
  • Discard to make a suit void so you can trump it.
