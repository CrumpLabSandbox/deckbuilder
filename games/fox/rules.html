The Fox in the Forest

Cheat sheet. Two players, about 30 minutes. Based on the game by Joshua Buergel.

A round

  1. 33 cards: Bells, Keys and Moons, 1 to 11. Deal 13 each. Turn up the next card as the decree: its suit is trump. The rest is the deck.
  2. Non-dealer leads. Follow suit if you can. Highest trump wins; with no trump, the higher card of the suit led wins. The winner leads next.
  3. After 13 tricks, score. Swap dealer. First to 21 at the end of a round wins (a tie plays another round).

Scoring

TricksTitlePoints
0–3Humble6
4 / 5 / 6Defeated1 / 2 / 3
7–9Victorious6
10–13Greedy0

Plus 1 point for each 7 (Treasure) in the tricks you won.

Odd cards

CardPower
1 SwanIf you play this and lose the trick, you lead the next trick.
3 FoxWhen you play this, you may exchange the decree card with a card from your hand.
5 WoodcutterWhen you play this, draw 1 card, then put any 1 card from your hand on the bottom of the deck.
7 TreasureThe winner of the trick gets 1 point for each 7 in the trick.
9 WitchIf this is the only 9 in the trick, treat it as a trump.
11 MonarchWhen you lead this, your opponent, if they have this suit, must play the 1 of it or their highest card of it.

Tips

  • Look at your hand and decide: Humble (few tricks) or Victorious (7–9). Both pay 6.
  • Stopping at 9 is hard with a hand full of winners; spend big cards early.
  • Watch your opponent's count. Pushing them into Greedy is as good as scoring yourself.
